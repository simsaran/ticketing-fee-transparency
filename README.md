# The Hidden Fee Problem
### Canadian Concert Ticketing Fee Transparency: Business Analysis and Project Plan

I bought a concert ticket recently. The price on the listing was $89. By the time I checked out I paid $340. I did not notice the fees building up until I saw the total. I just clicked through. I think most people do the same thing.

Ticketmaster recently introduced a rule in Canada stopping resellers from listing above face value. That is progress. But the original fee problem is still there. Service fee, facility fee, order processing fee, delivery fee — still hidden until the last screen. The resale price is now regulated. The fee transparency is not.

This project maps what is still broken and what fixing it actually looks like.

---

## What this is

A full business analysis and project plan for implementing a mandatory fee transparency standard across Canadian concert ticketing platforms. Five platforms assessed against a common baseline. Ten process steps mapped from event discovery to venue entry. Fifteen requirements written with MoSCoW prioritisation and acceptance criteria. Eight stakeholders analysed with influence ratings and engagement strategies. Ten risks identified with mitigation plans. A full 18-month project implementation plan with phases, milestones, and an audit framework.

---

## What the analysis found

Only one of the five major Canadian ticketing platforms shows fees to the consumer before they select their seats. The other four reveal the full price for the first time at the checkout screen after the consumer has waited in a virtual queue, created an account, selected their seats, and made a psychological commitment to the purchase.

| Platform | Face Value | Total Fees | Total Price | Fees Upfront |
|---------|-----------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Ticketmaster Canada | $89 | $45.25 | $134.25 | No |
| StubHub Canada | $89 | $43.65 | $132.65 | No |
| SeatGeek Canada | $89 | $36.20 | $125.20 | No |
| Eventbrite Canada | $89 | $22.79 | $111.79 | Partial |
| Fever Canada | $89 | $17.00 | $106.00 | Yes |

The average fee across all five platforms is 37% above face value. The range is 19.1% to 50.8%.

## The two deliverables

The BA report covers the current state process map showing all 10 steps from discovery to venue entry with the three non-value-added steps identified, the platform fee benchmarking table, and the requirements register summary with implementation status for each requirement.

The project plan covers the full stakeholder map with influence ratings and engagement strategies, the three-phase implementation plan across 18 months with milestones and success measures, the risk register with likelihood and consequence ratings, and the ongoing monitoring and audit framework.
